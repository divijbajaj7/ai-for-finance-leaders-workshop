# House style — monthly management commentary

## Structure (always in this order)
1. **Headline**: one sentence. "Group EBITDA of MUR X.Xm vs budget MUR Y.Ym, (MUR Z.Zm) A (−N.N%), driven by A, B and C."
2. **Divisional sections** in order of absolute EBITDA variance. For each material item:
   - **Driver:** what happened (volume, price, timing, one-off, mix). Only what the data shows.
   - **Impact:** the MUR effect and whether it is timing (reverses) or permanent.
   - **Action:** owner and next step, or "to confirm with <division>".
3. **Watch list**: 2–4 lines on items trending toward materiality.

## Conventions
- Units: MUR m, 1 decimal. Source data is MUR '000, so divide by 1,000.
- Favourable: `MUR 12.0m F`. Adverse: `(MUR 26.7m) A`. Percentages to 1 decimal.
- Timing variances are labelled "timing" and the expected month is given if known.
- Intercompany items: "eliminates on consolidation; no group impact".
- Plain, active sentences. No adjectives like "significant" without a number.
- ≤ 250 words total. No speculation, and never invent causes.

## Example (approved style)
**Headline:** Group EBITDA of MUR 356.0m vs budget MUR 366.9m, (MUR 10.9m) A (−3.0%), mainly higher coal cost in Energy partly offset by lower cane cost.

**Energy:** Coal costs (MUR 14.8m) A. *Driver:* higher coal price and more coal burnt as bagasse supply fell. *Impact:* permanent for the month. *Action:* Energy to present a coal procurement update at the next ExCo.

**Watch list:** ERP project spend running 40% over monthly budget. FX loss building on the USD loan as MUR weakens.
