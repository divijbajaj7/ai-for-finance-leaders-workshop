# Board deck — 8–10 slides (5 fixed + one per division with a material item) (16:9, Arial, navy #1E3A5F titles, blue #2563EB accents)

1. **Title**: entity, "Monthly Board Pack", period, "Budget vs Actual · MUR m". Navy background.
2. **Headline**: headline sentence + 4 KPI tiles (Revenue, Total costs, EBITDA, Net result), each with actual, budget and variance (green F / red A); materiality footnote.
3. **EBITDA bridge**: waterfall from budget EBITDA through each material line (sorted adverse → favourable) and "Other" to actual EBITDA.
4…n. **Divisional slides**: one per division with at least one material item, ordered by |EBITDA variance| (largest first). Left: table of material lines (Budget, Actual, Var). Right: Driver → Impact → Action bullets.
n+1. **Risks & watch list**: 3–5 bullets.
n+2. **Decisions & next steps**: numbered owner-based actions.

Rules: max ~40 words of body text per slide, every number from `result.json`, no orphan slides with no content.
