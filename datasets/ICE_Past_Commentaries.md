# ICE Group — Approved Management Commentaries (fictional, for training)

These are the two most recent commentaries approved by the Group Finance Director. They are examples of the house style.

---

## ICE GROUP — MONTHLY MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY — JULY 2026

**Headline:** Group EBITDA of MUR 331.1m was MUR 7.2m (2.1%) below budget. A stronger month in Cane & Sugar was more than offset by higher coal costs in Energy and FX losses at Corporate.

*Materiality applied: variances ≥ MUR 5.0m or ≥ 10% of budget. All figures vs budget; F = favourable, (A) = adverse.*

**Cane & Sugar — EBITDA MUR 160.3m vs MUR 157.2m: MUR 3.1m F**
- **Cost of cane: MUR 7.6m F.** *Driver:* cane crushed was 4.6% below budget (206k t vs 216k t) because dry conditions reduced yields. *Impact:* lower payments to planters, partly offset by lower sugar volumes. *Action:* Agronomy to issue a revised crop estimate by 15 August.

**Energy — EBITDA MUR 192.9m vs MUR 199.1m: MUR 6.2m (A)**
- **Coal costs: MUR 8.1m (A).** *Driver:* the average coal price was MUR 6,950/t against a budget of MUR 6,600/t, and less bagasse was available. *Impact:* margin pressure, because electricity export tariffs are fixed. *Action:* Procurement to present hedging options for the Q4 coal cargoes.

**Corporate — costs MUR 24.3m vs MUR 20.3m: MUR 4.0m (A)**
- No individual line above threshold.

**Watch list:** drought impact on the remaining crop; coal price trend; FX loss of MUR 3.3m on the USD term loan (below threshold, but USD/MUR moved from 45.20 to 45.35).

*Prepared by: Group FP&A · Reviewed by: Group Finance Director*

## ICE GROUP — MONTHLY MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY — AUGUST 2026

**Headline:** Group EBITDA of MUR 356.0m was MUR 10.9m (3.0%) below budget. Coal costs widened the gap in Energy, while Cane & Sugar again outperformed thanks to lower cane costs.

*Materiality applied: variances ≥ MUR 5.0m or ≥ 10% of budget. All figures vs budget; F = favourable, (A) = adverse.*

**Cane & Sugar — EBITDA MUR 193.4m vs MUR 188.2m: MUR 5.2m F**
- **Cost of cane: MUR 12.6m F.** *Driver:* cane crushed was 224.5k t against 240k t budgeted (−6.5%) as the drought continued. *Impact:* lower cane costs. Sugar sales were in line (−0.4%) because a higher price (MUR 22,790/t vs 21,500/t) offset lower volumes. *Action:* Sales to confirm Q4 contract pricing so volume risk is not doubled by price risk.

**Energy — EBITDA MUR 186.5m vs MUR 196.7m: MUR 10.2m (A)**
- **Coal costs: MUR 14.8m (A), 11.0% over budget.** *Driver:* the coal price was MUR 7,100/t vs 6,600/t (+7.6%), and more coal was burnt to make up for lower bagasse supply. *Impact:* the largest single adverse item in the month, and the trend is worsening. *Action:* Energy to model the cost of the coal-to-bagasse switch for the rest of the crop; Procurement hedging proposal to go to the September Exco.

**Corporate — costs MUR 26.3m vs MUR 20.3m: MUR 6.0m (A)**
- **IT & ERP project: MUR 1.4m (A), 40% over budget.** *Driver:* additional implementation consultancy days for the finance module. *Impact:* the cumulative overrun now needs a budget reforecast. *Action:* PMO to present a revised ERP budget to the September steering committee.

**Watch list:** FX loss of MUR 4.8m (below threshold) as USD/MUR moved to 45.60; drought impact on September crush; land sale of MUR 45m budgeted for September is still awaiting a land conversion permit.

*Prepared by: Group FP&A · Reviewed by: Group Finance Director*
